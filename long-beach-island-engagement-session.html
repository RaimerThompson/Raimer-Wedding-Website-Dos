Long Beach Island Engagement Session

Robyn + Jordan

L.B.I.

If you've never been to LBI then you have missed out on a small oasis of land, sea, and great hospitality. There's nothing like beach bumming it all day and through the night watching the sunrise one day and being mezmorized by its beauty has it engulfs the sky with light and warmth. Then you have the ever lasting longing for the day not to end as the sunsets and cast the most beautiful golden glow on to our faces and then chnages to the blue and later night sky and stars.

Robyn and Jordan cemented their lives together way before coming out to LBI. But Jordan wanted to create something special for Robyn who spent many of her childhood growing up in the same beaches we eneded up doing their engagement session in. To have photos to commemorate their love and life together was Jordan's way of ways remebering each moment together. They decided to bring along their beautiful pup Zeena to the session because she is an extension of their lives and every day they get to spend with her is a gift in on to itself as Z is getting up their in age.

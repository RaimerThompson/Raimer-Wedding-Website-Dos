Engagement Session like no other in Long Beach Island

If you've never been to LBI then you have missed out and a small oasis of land, sea, and great hospitality. There's nothing like beach bumming it all day and through the night watching the sunrise one day and being mezmorized by its beauty has it engulfs the sky with light and warmth. Then you have the ever lasting longing for the day not to end as the sunsets and cast the most beautiful golden glow on to our faces and then chnages to the blue and later night sky and stars.

Robyn and Jordan cemented their lives together way before coming out to LBI. But Jordan wanted to create something special for Robyn who spent many of her childhood growing up in the same beaches we eneded up doing their engagement session in. To have photos to commemorate their love and life together was Jordan's way of ways remebering each moment together. They decided to bring along their beautiful pup Zeena to the session because she is an extension of their lives and every day they get to spoend with her is a gift in on to itself as Z is getting up their in age.

Sunrise or Sunset for photos?

That was the question Jordan had for me as we were mapping out the engagement session and the possibilities we had. Sunrise while beautiful wasn't exactly the vibe he had described in what the moments he had envisoned to be captured. My recommendation was Sunset to maximize mins before Sunset and golden hour and then the few minutes after where there is still light to play with.

Your NYC engagement session in is your time to shine and get comfortable and not just being in front of the camera but by also forming a partnership and trust with your photographer.

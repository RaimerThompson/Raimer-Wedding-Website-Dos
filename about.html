Welcome, I'm Raimer, but everyone calls me Ray. My own wedding was the start of my photography journey. It was pivotal in me finding my true self, I literally said to myself that morning when I woke up "Im going to live this day to my fullest, without a care in the world of how I looked, moved, or danced" and it was truly one of the most special days of my life. Marrying my wife Grace was the best and smartest decision I've ever made, she's my bestfriend and my why in what I do.

I live my life every day giving as many positive vibes as I can to the world, and thats the attitude I bring to wedding day, engagement sessions, etc., I'm all about creating a bespoke experience, curated just for you and the most important people that will join you in your journey. Its truly my hope and mission that on your day you too can feel that you can live your day to the fullest. Here's your permission slip.
